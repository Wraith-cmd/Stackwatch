Why it cannot break on the pull

Since 12.1 no addon can read your stack count during a boss fight. StackWatch never tries. It sets up three Blizzard aura containers at login and then does nothing during combat. The game client draws the number, fills the bar and flips the colors.

baseicon, number, swipe, bar
cautionshown by Blizzard when stacks ≥ 5
dangershown by Blizzard when stacks ≥ 8

The orange and red layers are invisible StatusBars whose only job is to be shown or hidden by applications >= minApplications inside Blizzard's secure code. Their border, tint and number ride along.

Commands

/sw testdemo that cycles 0 to 10 stacks in the real spot /sw unlockdrag handle; then /sw lock /sw configsettings panel (also Esc > Options > AddOns) /sw statusraid-night diagnostics /sw allshow every debuff, not only boss debuffs /sw soundtick on every stack gained (off by default)

Install and share

  1. Unzip StackWatch-1.0.0.zip.
  2. Put the StackWatch folder in _retail_\Interface\AddOns.
  3. /reload, then /sw test.

For the guild: push the source to GitHub, tag v1.0.0, and the included workflow packages a release. Guildmates paste the repo link into WowUp and get updates automatically. Add a Wago or CurseForge token to the workflow to publish there too.

Cost on the PC

No OnUpdate, no combat log, no per-frame Lua in a fight. Four events, all out of combat. About 40 KB of Lua and one 4 KB sound. Rebuilds after a settings change happen out of combat only.

Raid-night fallback: if Eternal Venom does not appear on the first pull, Blizzard did not flag it as a boss aura. Type /sw all and it shows with every other debuff.
